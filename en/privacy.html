Privacy Policy

How we handle the personal data you send us through this website.

1. Data controller

The controller of the data collected on this website is S&S Abogados, with offices at Av. Alicia Moreau de Justo 1150, 3rd Floor, Office A306, CP 1107, Autonomous City of Buenos Aires, Argentina. Phone: +54 11 5278-3435.

2. What data we collect

Only the data you voluntarily send us: through the contact form, your name, email, phone number and the content of your inquiry; through «Work at S&S», also your résumé and any information it contains. The site does not require registration and does not collect sensitive data.

3. How we use it

To answer your inquiry, schedule a meeting and, in the case of job applications, to assess your profile for current or future openings at the firm. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or transfer it to third parties.

4. Confidentiality

The information you share is protected by the attorney–client privilege that governs the legal profession. Only the firm's team has access to it.

5. Retention

We keep your data for as long as needed for the purpose for which it was sent and, where applicable, for the periods required by law.

6. Your rights

Under Argentine Law 25,326 on Personal Data Protection, you may request access to, rectification, updating or deletion of your data by writing to us through the contact form. The right of access may be exercised at intervals of no less than six months, unless a legitimate interest is shown (section 14, subsection 3).

The Agency for Access to Public Information (Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública), as the supervisory authority of Law 25,326, is empowered to handle complaints and claims filed by anyone whose rights are affected by non-compliance with the personal data protection rules in force.

7. Cookies

This website does not use its own tracking cookies. Embedded third-party services (such as Google Maps or web fonts) may use their own, under their respective policies.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The version in force is always the one published on this page. Last updated: November 2026.
