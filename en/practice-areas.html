Practice Areas

01

Health Care Injunctions & Writs

We understand the urgency and critical importance of health-related matters and medical coverage. We provide legal representation and counsel to guarantee the effective fulfillment of medical benefits against healthcare providers and private health insurance companies. We are currently litigating cases involving:

  1. Access to Treatments and Medications: Filing amparo actions and summary proceedings with urgent preliminary injunctions to secure high-complexity medications, medical supplies, prostheses, specific therapies, and denied or delayed medical coverage.
  2. Continuity of Coverage Upon Retirement: Legal advice and representation to retain the same private health insurance or healthcare plan upon retirement, safeguarding tenure and coverage conditions without unjustified price hikes or forced transfers.
  3. Disability Rights and 100% Coverage: Claims for full comprehensive coverage of medical services for individuals with a Disability Certificate (CUD).
  4. Unfair Rate Increases and Denial of Enrollment: Claims against excessive or abusive fee increases outside regulatory limits, as well as enrollment denials due to pre-existing conditions.

Your health cannot wait; defending your right to receive the medical care you deserve is our priority.

02

Criminal Law

We provide legal counsel, representation, and defense in criminal matters, assisting our clients with maximum confidentiality, swiftness, and technical rigor through complex or critical situations. We design tailored strategies for each case to safeguard their rights and minimize legal exposure.

  1. Legal Counsel and Procedural Representation: We advise and represent clients across all stages of criminal proceedings, from the Preliminary Investigation phase to Oral Trials, as well as in the drafting and filing of Appeals, Cassation, and Extraordinary Appeals.
  2. Active Role as Private Prosecutor / Complainant: We work proactively alongside prosecutor offices representing victims. We take an active stance in criminal proceedings to supply key evidence and continuously drive the case forward.
  3. Criminal Defense Management: Our team includes specialists trained to provide defense representation across a wide range of criminal offenses:
    1. Offenses against the person (homicide, assault and aggravated assault, attempted offenses).
    2. Property crimes (robbery, theft, fraud).
    3. Offenses against personal liberty and physical integrity (criminal threats, unlawful detainment/false imprisonment, sexual offenses).
    4. Offenses against public administration, public order, and public safety.
    5. Offenses against public authority, constitutional order, and economic and financial systems.

When personal freedom and peace of mind are at stake, we provide strategic, rigorous, and highly confidential legal support.

03

Corporate Criminal Law & Compliance

In an increasingly regulated and interconnected corporate environment, managing legal risk is critical to a company's sustainability and reputation. We provide comprehensive legal counsel to domestic companies facing criminal and investigative risks, combining preventive strategies with specialized technical defense.

Practice Areas

  1. Corporate Defense and Prosecution: We represent companies as defense counsel or as private prosecutors in criminal proceedings involving economic crimes, corporate fraud, tax and banking offenses, cybercrime, and offenses against public administration.
  2. Internal Investigations and Digital Forensics: We design and conduct internal investigations into suspected or reported wrongdoing within the organization. We implement advanced protocols for the collection, chain of custody, and preservation of digital evidence, ensuring its admissibility in court.
  3. Compliance Programs and Preventive Advisory: We design and implement regulatory compliance and integrity programs tailored to the reality of each company. Our preventive approach helps to:
    1. Identify and classify sector-specific operational and legal risks.
    2. Establish internal prevention, monitoring, and response mechanisms through codes of conduct, ethics helplines, and action protocols.
    3. Mitigate critical contingencies such as reputational damage, severe financial penalties, business loss, or debarment from public tenders and government procurement.

Our Key Differentiator

The team leading this practice consists of highly experienced professionals with a distinguished background in the public sector. This comprehensive perspective enables us to understand institutional dynamics from within, delivering strategic, preventive, and highly effective solutions for complex disputes.

04

Insurance & Personal Injury / Traffic Accidents

With over 15 years of specialized experience in the sector, we provide legal counsel, representation, and advocacy in traffic accident cases and civil and criminal liability claims, aimed at securing fair compensation and damages.

Our Services

  1. Comprehensive Traffic Accident Management: Active legal representation against insurance companies and liable third parties in motor vehicle accidents.
  2. Compensation Claims: Effective legal actions to recover damages for property loss, physical and psychological disability, lost earnings (lucro cesante), pain and suffering (daño moral), and medical or repair expenses.
  3. Dual Civil and Criminal Practice: Our internal structure enables us to handle motor vehicle accidents simultaneously across civil claims for damages and criminal proceedings arising from vehicle operation (vehicular assault, bodily injury, or reckless homicide).
  4. Negotiation and Litigation Strategy: We lead out-of-court negotiations to secure prompt and favorable settlements; when an agreement cannot be reached, we aggressively and rigorously pursue claims through court litigation.

Our Added Value

To solidly establish and back every claim, we work closely with an interdisciplinary team of expert witnesses:

  1. Mechanical Engineers and Accident Reconstruction Experts: For the technical reconstruction of events and precise determination of collision mechanics.
  2. Forensic Medical Examiners and Psychologists: To scientifically assess the degree of physical impairment, psychological sequelae, and actual impact on the victim's life.

This technical alliance allows us to accurately quantify damages, build solid evidentiary foundations, and deliver high-quality results for our clients.

05

Probate, Estate Planning & Inheritance Law

We understand that transferring family wealth or dealing with the loss of a loved one involves high emotional weight and organizational complexity. Therefore, we approach every probate process with technical rigor, agility, and empathy, offering clear solutions to protect assets and preserve family harmony.

Our Services

  1. Probate and Estate Proceedings (Intestate and Testate): Efficient and prompt handling of probate proceedings in the Autonomous City of Buenos Aires and the Province of Buenos Aires. We manage all stages of the process, from obtaining the Declaration of Heirs (Declaratoria de Herederos) or validating a will, to the distribution and registration of real estate, registrable personal property, and financial assets.
  2. Preventive Estate Planning: Comprehensive legal counsel to structure the future transfer of assets during one's lifetime, optimizing costs, preventing future family disputes, and honoring the testator's wishes through:
    1. Drafting and registration of wills.
    2. Lifetime property gifts with reserved life estate (donación con reserva de usufructo vitalicio).
    3. Business succession planning and family business governance protocols.
    4. Partition agreements and trusts.
  3. Heir Dispute Resolution: Legal mediation, negotiation, and representation in inheritance disputes, clawback/collation actions (colación), reduction actions, challenge of wills, and exclusion of heirs.
  4. Testamentary Trusts: Design and implementation of testamentary trusts for the administration, protection, and deferred distribution of assets to heirs, individuals with disabilities, or family business management.

We safeguard the assets built over a lifetime of effort, facilitating an orderly, secure, and transparent transition for your loved ones.

06

Corporate & Commercial Law

We partner with companies, SMEs, and startups at every stage of their growth, delivering dynamic, strategic legal support tailored to business success. We provide effective solutions for both day-to-day operational decisions and high-complexity transactions or corporate dispute resolutions.

Our Services

  1. Monthly Retainer Services (Out-of-Court Advisory): A corporate retainer service designed for businesses seeking cost predictability and ongoing legal backing in their daily operations without resorting to court litigation. Includes:
    1. Continuous preventive legal counsel for executive decision-making.
    2. Drafting and reviewing formal legal notices (cartas documento) and official communications.
    3. Pre-litigation mediation and negotiations with suppliers, clients, or third parties.
    4. Ongoing legal risk auditing and regulatory compliance.
  2. Corporate Law & Governance: Business formation and incorporation, corporate charter amendments, capital increases, share/equity transfers, shareholder agreements, and administrative filings before public registries (IGJ).
  3. Complex Commercial Contracts: Structuring and negotiation of domestic and international contracts (franchising, distribution, leasing, trusts, licensing, and supply agreements).
  4. Commercial Dispute Resolution: Representation in corporate litigation, administrative proceedings, and arbitration tribunals when out-of-court negotiations are exhausted.

Our Key Differentiator

Our team consists of highly qualified professionals who combine technical excellence with deep industry knowledge. Having served in both the private and public sectors, our attorneys bring a unique dual perspective that represents a decisive strategic advantage—both at the negotiating table and in court.
