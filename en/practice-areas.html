Practice Areas

A team prepared for every legal need

We developed a structure that allows us to absorb large volumes of work with an optimum cost-quality balance, while also personally attending to those who require a smaller workload.

01

Criminal Law

We advise our clients in all stages of the process, during the investigation, Oral Trial, or through Appeal and other Resources.

Our firm is working alongside the prosecutors, intervening in various processes as a plaintiff and/or injured party, assuming a more active role within the framework of the criminal process in order to provide evidence and initiate the criminal case.

We currently manage defenses in: homicides, crimes against sexual integrity, theft and robbery, minor or aggravated injuries, attempts, threats and unlawful deprivation of liberty, crimes against public order, crimes against the security of the Nation, crimes against constitutional order, crimes against the public administration, and crimes against the economic and financial order.

Our firm has highly trained specialists in the matter, providing full advice on behalf of our clients in order to establish an adequate strategy that allows us to overcome and minimize the risks that may arise from critical situations.

02

Business Criminal Law and Compliance

Our firm represents local companies in a wide variety of criminal matters, both as defenders and as individual prosecutors.

We currently manage criminal defenses and complaints, as well as internal investigations, and the collection and preservation of digital evidence.

Our firm also offers preventive criminal advice through the implementation of Compliance programs, aimed at executing internal procedures and good practices. This methodology allows us to identify and classify the operational and legal risks our clients face, and then establish internal mechanisms for prevention, management, control and response.

The risks to be prevented are those that carry consequences such as reputational damage, the imposition of significant fines and penalties, business losses, or exclusion from public tenders or subsidies, among others.

Our team has vast experience in the area and has worked in the public sector, bringing a valuable combination of experience and knowledge.

03

Insurance and Accidents

We are specialists in traffic accidents and advise our clients in making claims against insurance companies or third parties in order to receive the corresponding compensation.

We have an interdisciplinary team of physicians, psychologists and mechanical engineers who assist us in the defense and better care of our clients; all of this allows us to provide comprehensive advice and offer quality results.

Our structure allows us to handle claims that give rise to civil and criminal liability arising from motor vehicle use. The firm has more than 10 years of experience in the sector, guaranteeing excellent work and total commitment on behalf of our clients.

04

Family Law and Succession

Our firm advises clients through divorce proceedings, dissolution of marital property, child support and custody arrangements, as well as estate planning and probate proceedings.

05

Commercial and Entrepreneur Counselling

Our firm advises clients on commercial, corporate and contract matters, representing local companies across a wide range of operations as well as in their clients' day-to-day legal affairs.

It has highly trained professionals in the area with deep knowledge of the industry they work for, which is an added value of high importance when defending a case before a judicial, administrative or arbitral tribunal.
